Privacy Policy

Namvandi Driver

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Athiva Technology LLP ("we", "our", "us") operates the Namvandi Driver mobile application. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Phone Number — collected during registration for OTP-based authentication
  • Precise Location — collected continuously during active trips and in the background to track routes and calculate mileage
  • Identity Documents — Aadhaar card and driving licence images collected during KYC onboarding
  • Trip Data — GPS coordinates, distance, duration, and timestamps of completed trips
  • Device Information — device type and OS version for app compatibility

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Track and log trip routes for accuracy and earnings calculation
  • Verify driver eligibility through KYC
  • Calculate rewards and trip payouts
  • Improve app performance and reliability

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Our backend servers to process trips and authentication
  • Government authorities if required by law

5. Data Retention

Trip and location data is retained for 2 years for earnings records and dispute resolution. Account data is retained until you delete your account.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at admin@athivatech.com.

7. Location Data

The app requires "Always On" location access to track trips accurately when the app is in the background. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings, but trip tracking will not function without it.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.
